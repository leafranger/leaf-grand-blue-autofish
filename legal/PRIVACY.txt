LEAF'S GRAND BLUE AUTOFISH - PRIVACY NOTICE

Contact: Mirko "Leafranger" Cisternino, leafrangerit@gmail.com

SHORT VERSION
Leaf's Grand Blue Autofish runs on your computer. It has no accounts, no analytics, no
telemetry and no crash reporting. Nothing about you or how you play is sent
to the author or to anyone else, so the author does not collect or process
your personal data.

WHAT HAPPENS ON THE INTERNET
- The app itself makes no network connections: no update check, no
  downloads, nothing sent. The one exception: if your PC lacks the Microsoft
  Visual C++ runtime, the first start offers to download it. Only if you click
  "Download and install it for me" does the app fetch Microsoft's official
  installer from aka.ms (Microsoft sees your IP address, as with any
  download), check that it is signed by Microsoft, and run it. Nothing is sent
  to the author.
- The Credits page and the optional support prompt contain links (Ko-fi,
  leafranger.dev). They open in your browser only when you click them. Those
  sites have their own privacy policies.

WHAT IT SAVES ON YOUR COMPUTER (inside the app's folder)
- Your settings and screen calibration (config, calibration profiles).
- Fishing history: catch log, saved sessions.
- Screenshots of the game window used to read the screen. These are processed
  in memory. They are only kept if you turn on the debug options (for example
  "Catch screenshots"), or when the app saves diagnostic pictures after a
  detection problem. Such pictures can show other players' names or chat if
  they are visible in the game window.
- Small local files for the app itself (theme, support-prompt answers).

None of this is uploaded. You can delete any of it at any time by deleting
the folders/files in the app's folder (sessions, recordings,
catch_samples, rarity_dataset, shakereports, shakecalib, and the *.json and
*.jsonl files). Deleting the whole app folder removes everything.
If you send a diagnostic report or screenshot to the author for support, you
are choosing to share it; check it for personal information first.

YOUR RIGHTS
Because the author holds none of your data, there is nothing to access or
erase on his side. If you have a privacy question, write to the contact
address above.
